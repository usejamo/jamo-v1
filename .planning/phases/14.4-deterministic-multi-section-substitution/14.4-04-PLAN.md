<objective>
Wire the multi-section fan-out into `AIChatPanel.tsx` — the three highest-risk seams of the phase. On a `substitute_placeholders` tool_result SSE: recognize it, group the model's `targets` by `section_key`, and for EACH section resolve+group+build edits (via Plan 01's pure functions) against that section's LIVE doc (`handle.getContent()` — client authoritative, D-04) and call `materializePendingEdits(bulkMsgId, sectionEdits)` with ONLY that section's edits. Reconcile every `MaterializeResult` + model/client skips into an honest outcome (D-15), store it on `toolData.state` so counts survive reload, render `BulkSubstitutionSummaryCard` (with accept-all/reject-all fanned across sections, D-14), and emit the client-composed chat one-liner (full/partial/zero-match, R7). Zero-match suppresses the card entirely (no empty panel).

The 14.3 per-section ghost-isolation guard runs inside each `materializePendingEdits` call — this is its FIRST real multi-section exercise (D-06). The guard is per-section and stateless; it passes because each edit's only declared block `data-id` equals its own `paragraph_id` anchor. The real failure mode is D-09 (two placeholders in one paragraph → competing replaces), already handled by Plan 01's grouping.

Purpose: R5 (fan-out review), R6 (aggregate wiring), R7 (honest reporting).
