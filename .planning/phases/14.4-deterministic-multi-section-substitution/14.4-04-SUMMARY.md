## Accomplishments
- A `substitute_placeholders` tool_result SSE is recognized (not dropped as unknown) and mapped to `tool-substitute-placeholders`
- One tool_result fans out into N per-section `materializePendingEdits` calls, each receiving only that section's edits, with section-namespaced edit ids (`${bulkMsgId}-${section_key}-${i}` via `buildSectionPendingEdits`)
- The 14.3 per-section ghost-isolation guard's first real multi-section exercise passes cleanly — each edit's anchor is scoped to its own section's live doc, resolved via `resolvePlaceholderTarget` (D-04)
- Applied/skipped counts are reconciled from the client outcome (`reconcileOutcome`) post-materialize and drive `BulkSubstitutionSummaryCard`, never the raw model claim (D-15)
- The chat emits a one-line summary on every run (`composeChatSummary`); zero-match shows a clear chat message and suppresses the aggregate card entirely (R7)
- Accept-all/reject-all fan `BATCH_ACCEPT_PENDING_EDITS`/`REJECT_PENDING_EDIT` across every affected section as coherent per-section batches (D-14)
- `BulkSubstitutionSummaryCard` now imports the real `SkipEntry` from `substitute.ts` instead of Plan 03's local shim
