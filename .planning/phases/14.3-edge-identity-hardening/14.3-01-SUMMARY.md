## Accomplishments
- `getAuthedUserAndOrg(req)` implements the canonical two-client pattern: anon+JWT client for `auth.getUser()` identity verification, service-role client for the `user_profiles.org_id` lookup — org_id is never trusted from JWT claims or body.
- Strict fail-closed guards: missing Authorization header, `authError || !user`, and null org_id all throw a 401 `Response` rather than proceeding with degraded/unsafe identity.
- `isInternalServiceRoleCall(req)` gives `retrieve-context` (plan 03) a way to preserve the trusted internal caller path (chat-with-jamo's service-role bearer) without blanket-requiring `auth.getUser()`.
- Unit tests cover all branches of the two pure functions (bearer match/mismatch/absent, jsonError status/body/CORS-merge).
