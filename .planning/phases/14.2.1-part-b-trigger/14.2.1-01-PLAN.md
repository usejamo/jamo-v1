<objective>
Extract the dead Part B trigger wiring (`AIChatPanel.tsx:228-242`) into a dedicated hook `useGapAnalysisTrigger` that implements the locked design from `14.2.1-CONTEXT.md`:

- **D-35:** On mount, if no `chat_sessions` row exists for `(proposal_id, user_id)`, fire `analyze-proposal-gaps` once with the current section summaries.
- **D-30:** Subscribe to Supabase Realtime UPDATE events on `proposal_sections` filtered by `proposal_id`. Debounce 3000ms after the last event. Then fetch the **full current** section content from the DB (NOT from `payload.new`), compute a content hash, skip if unchanged, otherwise invoke `analyze-proposal-gaps` with `run_id = crypto.randomUUID()`.
- Treat HTTP 429 as expected silence (the server's 30s cooldown rejects extra runs).

Purpose: keep the wiring testable, isolate the regression-prone debounce + hash logic, and avoid clumping a fourth `useEffect` into `AIChatPanel`. Wiring into `AIChatPanel` happens in Plan 02.

Output: the hook plus Vitest unit tests for the debounce, hash-skip, and 429-silence behaviors.
</objective>
