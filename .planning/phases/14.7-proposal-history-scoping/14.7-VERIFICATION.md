## Methodology note
Live non-draft content is thin: **won (2) and submitted (1) proposals have 0 chunks**; only the `lost` proposal `75a782a9…` (5 chunks) and drafts carry content. To exercise the status→switch mapping deterministically, the lost proposal's status was **morphed inside rollbacks** (never committed). The **vector RPC at `similarity_threshold=0.0`, `match_count=500`** returns *all* eligible chunks regardless of semantic match, so set-membership is exact (FTS text-matching would give false negatives).
