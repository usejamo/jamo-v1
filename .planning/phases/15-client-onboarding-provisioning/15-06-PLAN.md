<objective>
Build the org-admin backend: `team-invite` (same-org, role-capped teammate invites — reqs 6/8) and `team-manage` (same-org role change + deactivate/reactivate — req 7). Both derive the caller's org from the JWT and enforce that every operation targets the caller's OWN org, and that org admins can never mint `super_admin`. Deactivation uses the real authentication-blocking mechanism (`ban_duration`) plus the denormalized `is_active` flag (Pitfall 3).

Purpose: Tier-2 of the two-tier invite model (D-11) — org admins self-serve their teammates while the trust boundary stays server-enforced.
Output: 2 edge functions + Deno test scaffolds.
</objective>
