### Task 3 — E2E: ACCEPTED (with backlog 999.3 for one retrieval-strategy gap)

- **Positive path (grounded → asserts compliance)** — US, GCP-oriented query `"Good Clinical Practice requirements and responsibilities in clinical trial conduct"`: `regulatoryCount=5`, sources = **ICH-E6R3.pdf only** (geography scoping confirmed), assembled prompt has **ICH-GCP ×2** (base COMPLIANCE_CLAUSE + gated regulatory_strategy), `[REGULATORY CONTEXT]` populated with E6R3, **no** marker. ✓
