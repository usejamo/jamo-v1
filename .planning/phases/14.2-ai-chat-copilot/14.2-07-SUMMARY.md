## Summary

Implemented ActionQueue, ActionItem, ResumeTaskBanner, and WalkthroughProgress frontend components, then wired them into AIChatPanel, Sidebar, and ProposalDetail. Retired client-side `detectGaps()` behind a feature flag in favour of the new server-side `analyze-proposal-gaps` edge function.

- `ActionQueue.tsx` — collapse/expand, empty state, overflow footer, dismissed section, framer-motion animations; exports `QUEUE_ITEM_STATES`

- `WalkthroughProgress.tsx` — Stop walkthrough button, stage label, clarification copy
- `ResumeTaskBanner.tsx` — simpleHash drift detection, Restart/Discard inline prompt

**AIChatPanel.tsx** — imports ActionQueue + WalkthroughProgress; removes `gapCount`/`onGapsConsumed` props; removes direct `detectGaps` call

**Sidebar.tsx** — `pendingActionsCount` badge (orange pill on expanded, dot on collapsed)
