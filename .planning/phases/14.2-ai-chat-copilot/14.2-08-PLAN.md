   - Source paragraph unchanged
   - Ghost decoration disappears

7. Close and reopen the tab. Verify: EditSummaryCard shows the preserved tally (e.g., "1 accepted, 1 rejected"). Click "Review in editor →" — verify ghost decorations re-appear for any `pending` changes.
